# Join us

Botacin's Lab is looking for new **PhD, Master's and undergraduate** students.

<div class="feature-grid" markdown>

<div markdown>
### :material-school: PhD
PhD positions are typically funded. Apply through the university's graduate admissions process, then email Prof. Botacin. Self-funded PhD and postdoctoral candidates should contact him directly. Check the Texas A&M graduate admissions site for the application deadline.
</div>

<div markdown>
### :material-account-school: Master's
Enroll in the program first, then choose Prof. Botacin as your advisor.
</div>

<div markdown>
### :material-account-group: Undergraduate
Undergraduate researchers are welcome, including voluntary positions.
</div>

</div>

## Contact

Email **[botacin@tamu.edu](mailto:botacin@tamu.edu)** and tell us what you want to work on. A short note about the research areas from the [home page](index.md#research-areas) that interest you helps.

!!! warning "Official contact only"
    The lab does not recruit by non-Texas A&M email or by WhatsApp. Inquiries should come through Texas A&M channels.

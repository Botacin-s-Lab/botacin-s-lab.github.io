### Research interests
- Malware analysis, evasion and detection
- Sandbox development
- Antivirus operations
- Hardware-assisted security
- Reverse engineering

### Research topics (from the research page)
- **Malware:** longitudinal analyses of in-the-wild threats, static and dynamic detection, sandboxes, analysis frameworks, fuzzing and symbolic execution of samples.
- **Debuggers:** new debuggers, how analysts use debuggers to reverse engineer code, AI-enhanced debuggers.
- **Anti-virus:** metrics for evaluating real solutions, next-gen designs.
- **AI/ML:** ML malware detection, evaluation in real scenarios, adversarial ML against detectors, AI-generated malware.
- **Hardware security:** moving antivirus capabilities into hardware, secure-by-design systems.
- **Theory and methods:** formal definition of malware and theories of maliciousness, evaluation metrics, bibliographic analysis, OS resilience and self-healing, malware clustering and threat intelligence extraction.

### Funding
- **NSF Award #2327427**, SaTC: CORE: Small. PI: Marcus Botacin. Hardware Performance Counters (HPC) as next-generation malware detection. Three goals: minimum number of HPCs needed for a real CPU design, HPC vulnerability to mimicry attacks via compiler-based code diversity, and explainable AI models for HPC-level detection.
